How to play Tangram

Fill the dashed silhouette using all seven tans — two large triangles, one medium triangle, two small triangles, a square and a parallelogram. Pieces must not overlap, and every piece is used exactly once. The same seven pieces make every picture; only the silhouette changes.

Moving pieces

  • Drag a piece with your finger or mouse. Drop it back over the tray to return it to its tray slot.
  • Click or tap a piece to rotate it 45° — there is no Rotate button. Clicking a piece on the board turns it in place; clicking a tray piece lifts it onto the board and turns it there. A placed piece can be lifted and moved again — only Reset restarts the whole puzzle.
  • Flip mirrors a piece — it only changes the parallelogram, the one chiral piece. Flipping a triangle or the square changes nothing, so Flip is disabled for them.
  • Snap on drop: when a piece is close to its true spot (inside the dashed magnet circle), with the right rotation — and the right flip for the parallelogram — it snaps exactly into place. Turn the toggle off to place pieces fully by hand.

Modes

  • Classic counts up. There is no way to lose — take as long as you like.
  • Countdown 120 gives you 120 seconds. Reaching 0:00 shows a lose banner with an instant reset.

Hint and Peek

Hint glows the next unplaced piece and the slot it belongs in. Peek shows the finished picture, semi-transparent, for a few seconds. Both are unlimited in this demo.

Stars — the exact rule

  • 3 stars = solved with no Hint, no Peek, no wrong placement, and finished before the par time (par: Pyramid 30s, House 45s, Cat 60s, Swan 60s, Dog 75s).
  • 2 stars = no Hint and no wrong placement.
  • 1 star = solved.
  • "Perfect!" appears only for a true 3-star perfect solve, never otherwise.

Keyboard

  • Tab or click selects a piece. Selecting a tray piece with the keyboard lifts it onto the board.
  • R rotate clockwise · Shift+R counter-clockwise · F flip (parallelogram only) · H hint.
  • Arrow keys nudge the selected piece 1 unit (Shift + arrows: 0.25 units). Enter confirms — it tries to snap the selected piece into its slot.
