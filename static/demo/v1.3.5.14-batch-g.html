How this baseline plays
  1. Only the top card of each stack can be played. The layers underneath stay covered.
  2. Reveal tops from two different stacks. Equal symbols form a pair.
  3. Press Continue to lift a match and uncover the next layer. A mismatch closes without moving either stack.
  4. Cancel a single reveal freely. Clear all four stacks to finish.
