How to play

  1. Tap a cell, then tap a digit. Fill the grid so every row, column and box holds 1–9 exactly once — plus the extra rule for the variant you picked above.
  2. Notes toggles pencil marks. Placing a digit clears that note from its row, column and box automatically.
  3. A duplicate is refused — if a digit already sits in the row, column or box, it won't go in and it doesn't count as a mistake.
  4. A wrong digit counts. A digit that fits the row, column and box but isn't the solution is placed, marked red, and counts as a mistake. Three mistakes end the run.
  5. Hint fills one correct cell. Undo is free and unlimited.
  6. Watch the board. A placed digit pops in, a wrong one shakes, a refused duplicate shakes the cell, and a hint glows teal. When a row, column or box completes, it sweeps teal — and a finished grid ripples while your score counts up. All motion switches off if your device is set to reduce motion.

Scoring in this demo follows the variant matrix proposal (not yet locked): win 1,000 points, −1 per second floored at 200, −100 per hint, −50 per mistake, −50 per restart. Countdown scores 1,000 + 1 per second left instead of the per-second deduction. A hint-solved win is labelled “solved with hint”. Difficulty is graded by the hardest technique a board needs, not by clue count — Easy means singles and locked candidates only, about 5–10 minutes. Killer Easy grading and the 600-second clock are the two riskiest Batch A items in the matrix, so try those two hardest.
