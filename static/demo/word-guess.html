How the colours work

Right letter, right place Letter is in the word, wrong place Letter is not in the word

If a letter appears twice in your guess but only once in the answer, only one tile lights up — the other stays grey, meaning there is no second copy. Correct tiles are locked in first, then “in the word” tiles are given out left to right.

Hard Mode (set it before your first guess): letters shown in the right place must stay there, and letters shown as “in the word” must be used again in every later guess. A guess that ignores a revealed letter or a hint is not accepted.

Hint: up to 3 per round. A hint fixes one correct letter in its place on the board.

Scoring — proposal, not locked

Classic word-guess games award no points, just the result out of 6. For Morphi, the proposal is: a win scores 100 in 1 guess, 90 in 2, 75 in 3, 60 in 4, 45 in 5, 30 in 6. Each hint costs 100 points (score never drops below 0). Classic mode times you counting up; Countdown gives you 120 seconds. The owner still has to lock this formula.
