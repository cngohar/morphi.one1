# REPLAY_TEST.md

A/B replay test. This is a **qualitative usability and replay pilot**, not statistical proof: it can reveal confusion and obvious replay differences. It runs in Phase 2 on a private, unlisted preview (it needs non-owner testers); Phase 1B only builds the tooling. Recruit 5 to 10 people who are not the owner. They play with no instructions, about half starting with Memory and half with block placement.
