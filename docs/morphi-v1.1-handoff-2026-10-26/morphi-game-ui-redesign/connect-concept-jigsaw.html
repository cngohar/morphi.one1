CONCEPT 6 · THE JIGSAW — the board puts itself together. A puzzle site, so the board itself is a jigsaw: thirty-six tiles with real knobs and holes, dealt face-up but scattered. They fly in one by one and click into the tray — and the very last piece to land is the golden centre piece; only when it clicks does the name appear, because only then is the picture complete. Play your round; on Next the tiles lift out, shuffle in the air, and put themselves together again in new colours. Every deal, Morphi literally assembles your puzzle in front of you.
