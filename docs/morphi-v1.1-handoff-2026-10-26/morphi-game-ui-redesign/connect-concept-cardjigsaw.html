CONCEPT 8 · THE DEAL, ASSEMBLED — The Card × The Jigsaw. Your two favourites in one story. A face-down card slides in — nobody knows what it is. It flips: a jigsaw piece, and the name appears. Then the card unfolds and bursts into pieces: the tray fades in underneath and thirty-six jigsaw tiles rain into place, clicking together outside-in, the golden centre piece last — the picture completes itself. Play the round; at the end the jigsaw breaks apart, the pieces swirl back together and fold into a card again, which files itself onto your played stack — while the next face-down card is already waiting. Dealt as a card, played as a jigsaw, kept as a card.
