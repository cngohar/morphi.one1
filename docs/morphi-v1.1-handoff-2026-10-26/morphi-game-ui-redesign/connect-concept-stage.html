CONCEPT 4 · THE STAGE — tonight's puzzle, performed live. The house lights dim. A title card announces the act — and until that moment, not even the header knows what is playing. The curtains part, the spotlight finds the board, and the puzzle takes the stage. Play, take a bow (watch for the applause), and the curtains close — then reopen on Act II: the same stage, a new production, warm colours. Every deal is a performance with a beginning and an end; the puzzle is not a page, it is a show you are seated for. The shell is the theatre: header the marquee, bottom panel the orchestra pit.
