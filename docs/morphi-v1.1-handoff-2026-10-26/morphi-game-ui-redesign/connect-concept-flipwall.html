CONCEPT 7 · THE FLIP WALL — the board is dealt in clattering tiles. The whole screen is a split-flap board, like the old station departure wall. Press Play and all thirty-six tiles start flapping through symbols — clack-clack-clack — settling one by one until the puzzle stands revealed. Even the name is not shown: it is spelled, letter by letter, on flap tiles in the header. Next puzzle? The wall flaps again and deals a new board in new colours. Nothing on Morphi would ever sit still: the site itself deals with a sound and a shiver.
