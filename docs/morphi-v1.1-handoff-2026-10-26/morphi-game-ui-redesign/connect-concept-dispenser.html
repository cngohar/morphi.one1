CONCEPT 5 · THE DISPENSER — the machine deals you one. Morphi's selector is invisible; here it gets a body. A capsule drops out of the deal machine, rolls down the chute — and cracks open in the middle of the stage. That is the reveal: the name appears, and the puzzle's pieces spill out of the capsule and bounce into place, the board forming around them. Play your round, and when you are done the machine takes its pieces back — they stream up into the hopper, and the next capsule is already waiting its turn. Nobody can see what is inside a capsule. That is rather the point of Morphi.
