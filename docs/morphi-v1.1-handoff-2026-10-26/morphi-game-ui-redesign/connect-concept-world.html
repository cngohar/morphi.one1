CONCEPT 3 · THE SUSPENDED WORLD — every deal is a little place. The puzzle does not sit on the page; it floats in a small world made of three depths: the scattered pieces far away in soft mist, the board suspended in the middle, and large blurred pieces drifting close to you. Move your mouse (or drag) over the screen — the layers shift against each other, like looking through a window. On Next, the world dissolves and a different one condenses — new colours, new air (watch the second deal: it arrives warm). The shell stays put; what changes is that every deal now has weather.
