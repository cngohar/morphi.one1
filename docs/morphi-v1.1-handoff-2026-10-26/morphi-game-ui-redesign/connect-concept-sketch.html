CONCEPT 2 · THE LIVING SKETCH — the board draws itself. The screen is one sheet of paper and an invisible hand draws the round in ink: the grid strokes itself in, line by line; the dots are drawn pair by pair and washed with colour; the path is a real ink line. A slip leaves an ink blot — and out comes the eraser to rub it away (that is the backtrack). Finish, and the hand signs the corner while the score writes itself up. Ink colour, paper tone and the wobble of the hand are all procedural, from the deal's seed — no two boards are even drawn alike. The shell, gaps and bottom panel stay exactly where they are; only the rendering language changes.
