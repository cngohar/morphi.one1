CONCEPT 1 · THE CARD — the deal, made literal. A face-down card slides in (nobody knows what it is — not even the header: the name only appears at the flip). The card flips, shows its face, then unfolds into the board itself — the card IS the puzzle. You play the round (one path shown; the score runs up as if the round was played), and at the end the board folds back into a card and files itself onto the played stack at the right — a little deck that grows all session, your last score on top. Then the next face-down card is already waiting. The accepted shell is unchanged; only the puzzle's arrival and departure are physical.
