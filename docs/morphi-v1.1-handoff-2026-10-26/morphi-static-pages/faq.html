QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Frequently asked questions

Short answers, no spoilers. If your question is “which puzzle will I get?” — that is the one thing Morphi.One will not tell you in advance.

Do I need an account to play?

No. Press Play and you play as a guest — no sign-up, no download. Your high scores are kept on your own device.

Can I pick which puzzle I play?

No — and that is the point. There is no menu, catalogue or list to pick from. Press Play and a puzzle is dealt to you. Choosing would spoil the surprise the whole site is built around.

Will I get the same puzzle twice in a row?

Never. Every deal is fresh, and the deal never repeats the same kind twice in a row. Press Next after a round and a different puzzle arrives.

How do high scores work?

Each kind of puzzle keeps its own high score — shown as “Your high score” — saved on your device only. Scores are not blended across kinds into one total, and a brand-new kind starts at 0 with a NEW tag until you have played it.

Is Morphi.One free? Do I need to download anything?

Yes, it is free, and no download is needed. It runs in your browser, on your phone or computer, at morphi.one.

What if I do not like the puzzle I am dealt?

Finish the round and press Next — a different puzzle is dealt. There is no way to browse ahead or to request a particular one, because that would be a menu by another name.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes — Morphi.One is phone-first. Puzzles, boards and buttons are designed for a phone screen first, and they scale up to larger screens from there.

Why is there no list of all the puzzles?

Because a list would spoil the deal. The moment every puzzle is listed and reachable, pressing Play stops being a surprise. So the site explains how the deal works — on the How it works page — without listing what can be dealt.

Still curious?

The fastest answer is one press away — and it stays a surprise until it lands.
