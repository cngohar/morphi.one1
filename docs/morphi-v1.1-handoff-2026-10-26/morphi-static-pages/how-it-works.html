Design demo — the interactive “How it works” page. The infographic is the page’s centrepiece: it auto-plays through the four steps, and every step card is clickable (clicking pauses the auto-play; “Auto-play” resumes it). The animation is Anime.js, the boards are abstract on purpose — the page explains the deal, it never shows or names the puzzles themselves. Reduced-motion: the steps switch instantly with no motion. The page is assembled from the shared block kit (header · title · stage · step cards · Play band · footer), so its words can later be edited from the admin panel like the other static pages.
