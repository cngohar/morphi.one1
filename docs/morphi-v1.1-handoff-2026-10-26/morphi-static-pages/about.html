About Morphi.One

Morphi.One is a small, phone-first puzzle site built around one idea: you should not have to choose a puzzle to enjoy one. Press Play, and a puzzle is dealt to you — fresh every round, never the same kind twice in a row.

What it is

A place to play a quick puzzle, on your phone or your computer, with nothing to download and no account needed to start. Each round is drawn fresh, so the puzzle you get, how it looks, and how it is laid out are all part of the deal — not something you picked from a list.

Why there is no menu

If you could browse every puzzle in advance, the surprise would be gone before you pressed Play. So there is no catalogue to scroll through and no puzzle to select. The deal is the game: press Play, see what arrives, play it, then press Next for a different one.

What Morphi.One is — and is not

  • It is free to play, in your browser, with no download.
  • It is phone-first: every puzzle is designed to fit a phone screen.
  • It is scored per puzzle kind — your high score for a kind is kept on your own device.
  • It is not a catalogue of games, and it does not ask you to pick one.
  • It is not a download, an app store listing, or a copy hosted somewhere else — it lives here, at morphi.one.

A work in progress, on purpose

Morphi.One is still growing. New puzzles join the deal over time, and the way puzzles look keeps improving. What does not change is the deal itself: one Play, one fresh puzzle, no choosing, no spoilers.

Ready when you are.

Your first puzzle is one press away — and it stays a surprise until it lands.
