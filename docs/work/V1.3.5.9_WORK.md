### Implementation Summary

1. **10 Genuine Ruleset Mechanics (`src/lib/puzzles/memory/logic.ts`):**
   - **`memory:superstorm` (Extreme Thermal Weather Hazard):** Every 4 mismatches, an intense planetary storm sweeps the board, frosting over and locking the active row for 1 turn (preventing card flips in that sector).
   - **`memory:sentinel-heat` (Sentinel Wanted Level & Drone Lock):** Each mismatch increases Sentinel heat (1 to 3 stars). At 3 stars, a Sentinel drone locks the last flipped card face-down (cannot be flipped for 2 turns). A clean match cools heat down by 1 star.
   - **`memory:glyph-chain` (Atlas Glyph Sequence):** Ancient Portal Glyphs must be activated in strictly ascending sequence tier order (Tier 1 → Tier 2 → Tier 3 → ...). Cards can be flipped and remembered anytime, but matching pairs only clear when their active sequence tier is reached.
   - **`memory:analysis-visor` (Spectral Visor Scan):** Multi-tool Analysis Visor scans card backs, revealing glowing spectral category bands (Flora = Green, Fauna = Orange, Mineral = Blue, Tech = Purple) providing partial category intelligence before flipping.
   - **`memory:life-support` (Exosuit Life Support):** Starts at 100% Exosuit power. Each flip drains 3% power; matching pairs recharges +12%. Reaching 0% power causes catastrophic Exosuit Power Failure (Loss).
   - **`memory:hyperdrive-warp` (Warp Jump / Anomaly Shift):** Deck designates a Space Anomaly pair. When successfully matched, space-time warps: all remaining unmatched cards cyclically transpose across diagonal quadrants.
   - **`memory:gravitino-risk` (Gravitino Ball / High Stakes):** Contains 1 glowing Gravitino Orb. Pairing it immediately on the next flip grants +1000 bonus points; mismatching deducts 400 points and scrambles that column.
   - **`memory:pulse-drive` (Pulse Boost Speed Multiplier):** Consecutive matches scored in rapid succession (within 3.0 seconds) engage Pulse Drive, multiplying match score (2x → 3x → 4x). Pausing drops back to 1x sub-light.
   - **`memory:derelict-fog` (Derelict Freighter / Flashlight Cone):** Grid is in deep shadow (Fog of War). Flipping a card turns on an Exosuit flashlight, casting an illumination beam that reveals adjacent orthogonal neighbors.
   - **`memory:atlas-terminal` (16 / 16 Loop / Reset Pulse):** Atlas simulation loop ticks down: a terminal counter triggers a reality reset pulse every 16 flips, reshuffling face-down cards.

2. **Policy Dimensions & Mappings (`src/lib/puzzles/memory/dimensions.ts`):**
