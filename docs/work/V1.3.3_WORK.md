## Implemented scope

- Member Support at `/account/support`: own-ticket list, create, chronological
  conversations, reply, close, unread staff-reply badges and twenty-row pagination.
- Staff inbox at `/admin/support`: admin/moderator ticket handling and admin-only
  category/priority create, rename and delete/reassign. Protected General/Normal
  defaults cannot be renamed or deleted. The existing support operator role
  receives no staff ticket permission.
- Minimal D1 migration `0014_member_support.sql`: account-owned tickets/messages,
  per-ticket read markers, stable taxonomy ids, retry receipts and successful-write
  limiter events. Conditional receipt claims gate lifecycle, ownership, staff role,
  open state, live taxonomy and exact rolling quotas within the write transaction.
- Same-origin bounded JSON, owner-key binding, existing operator two-factor and
  recent-auth checks, private/no-store/noindex responses. Staff JSON stays under
  `/admin/support/data` to preserve the existing `/admin` operator-cookie scope.
- Plain escaped text and line breaks, no automatic link conversion. Lengths use
  trimmed UTF-16 units; subject 120, message 4,000, taxonomy name 60; NUL rejected.
  JSON bodies are bounded to 32,768 bytes including streamed requests.
- Quotas: three creates/hour, five open tickets, ten member replies/ten minutes,
  thirty staff replies/minute. Successful-write counts and content-free audits
  commit with the operation. Persistent retry receipts survive limiter cleanup.
  Staff quota rows do not cascade from the member's ticket/account deletion.
- Banned members retain support while gameplay checks remain unchanged. Deleted
  and pending-deletion accounts are denied support. Account deletion cascades to
  owned tickets and every message. Explicit maintenance prunes expired limits.
- Current-page memory holds failed drafts and retry ids; no browser persistence,
  email notifications or conversation content in audit/log/analytics/URL fields.
  Conversations open oldest first; a new reply may appear on a later page.
- Local privacy candidate, retention/request instructions, CODEMAP and decisions
  updated. No public policy publication or account deployment.
