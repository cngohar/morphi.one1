### Implementation Summary

1. **10 Genuine Ruleset Mechanics (`src/lib/puzzles/memory/logic.ts`):**
   - **`memory:cross-pollination` (Split Dual-Garden Baseline):** The board is partitioned into two physically distinct beds: Meadow Pod (Zone A) and Pollinator Grove (Zone B). Intra-garden flips are prohibited (`effect: 'ignored'`); matching pairs must span across the garden divide.
   - **`memory:mycelium-network` (Underground Spore Symbiosis):** Successful matches emit an underground mycelium pulse along matching row coordinates, revealing a brief fungal luminescence hint across connected roots.
   - **`memory:photosynthesis` (Sunlight Absorption & Sunbeam Rotation):** Sunlight cascades down one column at a time (`sunbeamRow`), shifting on mismatches. Pairs harvested under direct sunlight yield solar bonus energy.
   - **`memory:seed-dispersal` (Autumn Wind Breeze Scatter):** Mismatches trigger a gentle cross-bed breeze that swaps two hidden unmatched seeds between Meadow and Grove, redistributing botanical positions.
   - **`memory:seasonal-bloom` (Perennial Wilt Dormancy):** Repeatedly missed flowers wilt for 2 turns into dormancy (cannot be re-flipped until morning thaw).
   - **`memory:bee-waggle` (Honeybee Hive Waggle Dance):** A honeybee scouts the garden: its flight vector pinpoints which row contains the nectar-bearing match for the selected flower.
   - **`memory:carnivorous-briar` (Thorn Entanglement & Dewdrop Release):** Briars lock aggressive cards until matching clear dewdrop blossoms dissolve the entanglement.
   - **`memory:chlorophyll-meter` (Sap & Dewdrop Energy Budget):** Start with 100 Sap points. Flips consume 3 sap; mismatches wither 7 sap; matches restore 15 sap. Reaching 0 sap causes the garden to wither.
   - **`memory:bioluminescent` (Moonlight Phosphor Spore Symmetry):** Card backs emit phosphorescent spore patterns (even vs odd spore counts) providing subtle biological cues.
   - **`memory:botanical-graft` (Biodiversity Cross-Grafting Combos):** Consecutive cross-species pairings accumulate graft resonance multipliers (1x → 2x → 3x) that amplify scoring.

2. **Policy Dimensions & Mappings (`src/lib/puzzles/memory/dimensions.ts`):**

   - Mapped all 10 variants to their respective dimensions and archetypes (`information`, `pressure`, `core`, `constraints`, `grouping`, `dynamics`).
