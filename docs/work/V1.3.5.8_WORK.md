### Implementation Summary

1. **10 Genuine Ruleset Mechanics (`src/lib/puzzles/memory/logic.ts`):**
   - **`memory:countdown` (Countdown timer):** Introduces a per-round time limit (45s/60s/75s depending on pair count); exceeding limit transitions round status to `'lost'`.
   - **`memory:three-strikes` (Three strikes survival):** Player starts with 3 lives; each mismatch deducts 1 life; reaching 0 lives transitions round status to `'lost'`.
   - **`memory:wild-card` (Wild card):** Exactly one card is marked `isWild: true`; flipping it matches any card flipped immediately before or after it, with visual rainbow/wild treatment.
   - **`memory:sonar` (Sonar reveal ping):** Every successful match triggers a radar ping pulse that briefly reveals adjacent orthogonal neighbors (`sonar: number[]`).
   - **`memory:zebra` (Zebra bicolor constraint):** Cards are designated dark or light (`zebra: 'dark' | 'light'`); pairs only match when both cards share the symbol AND alternating colors (one dark + one light).
   - **`memory:drift` (Row drift):** On mismatch, unmatched cards in the row of the second flip cyclically shift by 1 position (`driftRow`).
   - **`memory:decay` (Seen card decay / fading clue):** Cards revealed during a mismatch start with a decay counter of 2 (`decay[idx] = 2`); on subsequent mismatches, decay decrements; when reaching 0, the card cannot be flipped for 1 turn.
   - **`memory:one-flip` (Single-flip continuity):** Only 1 card is flipped per turn; matching occurs against the single currently visible card from the previous turn.
   - **`memory:combo-burst` / `memory:combo` (Streak multiplier combo):** Consecutive matches increase `combo` multiplier (up to 3x); a mismatch resets combo to 1x.
   - **`memory:hazard` (Hazard trap card):** One pair is designated as hazard traps (`isHazard: true`); mismatching a hazard card triggers an immediate reshuffle of all unmatched cards.
