## 2. Rules — from global research

Source: global word-game research (13 Nov 2026, `workspace/research_notes/word-game-global-research-20261113/report.md`). The rules below are this brief's own statements; no external file is required to build from them.

- **Core loop:** guess one hidden 5-letter word in up to 6 guesses; each valid guess is submitted with Enter, tiles flip to feedback, and the player uses that feedback for the next guess until all five tiles are correct (win) or all six rows are used (lose, answer revealed). Solo letter-feedback guessing.
- **Feedback (count-aware, the rule that must be exact):** correct letter in the correct position = teal; letter in the word but wrong position = orange-red; otherwise grey. Duplicate handling: correct-position matches first (each exact match uses up one copy in the answer), then present matches left to right only while unmatched copies remain, then surplus copies are grey. A grey duplicate means "no further copy", not "letter absent". Demo-verified cases: PAPER vs APPLE, BREAD vs BERRY, PUPPY vs PEARL, ERROR vs BERRY.
- **Valid guesses:** a guess must be a real 5-letter word from Morphi's own guess list. Invalid words are rejected and do NOT spend a row (demo-verified with XXXXX). Two lists: a small curated answer list and a larger allowed-guess list; a word can be a legal guess without ever being a possible answer. Morphi curates its own lists — never copy NYT's lists (research §7).
- **Answer curation:** familiar words only; no simple S/ES plurals, no simple -ED past tenses, no proper nouns, no offensive or news-sensitive words (research §1/§4). Repeated letters allowed in answers and guesses.
- **Win:** all five tiles correct within 6 guesses. **Lose:** 6 guesses used, answer revealed, result X/6 (L4).
- **Hard Mode:** revealed correct letters must stay in place and present letters must appear in later guesses; guesses ignoring a confirmed hint are rejected (demo-verified: MUSIC rejected). Grey letters CAN be reused in Hard Mode (live-test evidence, research §3) — do NOT import a no-grey-reuse rule here. Must be enabled before the first guess.
- **Ultra Hard:** present letters must be reused in a *different* position and grey letters cannot be reused. Distinct from Hard Mode — do not combine the two rules into one preset.
- **Hint (hint-budget-3 variant only — Classic has NO hint; Revision 2 fix):** in the hint-budget-3 variant, a hint reveals one correct letter in its correct position, max 3 hints per round (L4 scoring charges −100 per hint). The hint is deterministic: the leftmost unrevealed correct position on the first unsolved board (§4). A hint never places a guess. Multi-board: a hint reveals on the first unsolved board (Morphi definition — no source defines a multi-board hint).
- **Controls:** physical keyboard (type A–Z, Enter submits, Backspace deletes) and on-screen QWERTY below the board (tap letters, Enter, Backspace/Delete). On-screen keys mirror the strongest feedback seen for each letter, in Morphi colours. High-contrast is an accessibility setting, not a variant.
- **Style reference:** Morphi branding is pale `#eef2f7`, navy `#1b2433`, Nunito, orange-red `#e5532d`, blue `#2f6fed`, teal `#009a86`, violet `#7d61ae`, vector only. Feedback colours are teal/orange-red/grey — NOT NYT green/yellow. Do NOT copy any existing game's art, icon or word list.

## 4. Generation, determinism, fairness

- **RNG:** `createRng(seed)` (`src/lib/engine/rng.ts`, SplitMix64 v1 — verified at HEAD `fa6279d`: `createRng(seed: string)` with `nextU64()`; `shuffle(rng, items)` exported from the same file, used by Connect). All draws from one stream in fixed order. Integer-only. **Banned in logic/manifest:** `Math.random`, `Date.now`, `performance.now`, `new Date`, float math (`Math.sqrt/pow/sin/...`). Enforced by ESLint + `scripts/check-boundaries.ts`; a float solver diverges across the 6 runtimes.
- **Word lists (NEW — no list exists in the repo today; verified by search at HEAD `fa6279d`):** Codex curates Morphi's own lists as data in the family folder (§8 item 16) — never NYT's lists. Source: the public-domain ENABLE word list (~172,823 words; 8,614 five-letter words, via `enable1.txt`) filtered by length, with commonness graded by SCOWL tiers (common = SCOWL size ≤35) — then Morphi's own editorial curation on top. Per length (4, 5, 6, 7): an answer list and a guess list (every answer is also a legal guess). Curation rules (§2): familiar words only; no simple S/ES plurals, no simple -ED past tenses, no proper nouns, no offensive or news-sensitive words. Answer-list size is brief-specified, not owner-locked (§10): at least 365 answers per length, so a daily word need not repeat within a year. The demo's lists (32 answers, ~350 guesses) are the seed for the 5-letter lists, not the production lists — they must be grown to the minimum above.
- **Daily word derivation:** the answer index is drawn from the seed stream (first `nextU64()` modulo answer-list length for the variant's length), so the same seed always yields the same word and every player faces the same word that day. The variant is fixed ONCE from attempt 0's derivation and never changes across the seed walk (§4, dailySeedOk below).
- **Solver gate (NEW — no word solver exists in the repo; the pattern is Nonogram's line-solver / Klondike's solver: enumerate/check/reject, not the algorithm):** Codex writes a deterministic solver in `logic.ts` — a greedy information-gain solver with fixed tie-breaks over the guess list, integer-only, running at generation time only (inside `dailySeedOk`), never during play. Every answer must be solvable in ≤6 guesses by that solver (the optimal-solver bound is ≤5, research §4: MIT's solver solves every curated answer in at most 5). Reject and regenerate (with an offset seed) any answer the solver cannot solve in 6.
- **Fairness gates (the hard rule — reject and regenerate on any failure):** a word is issued only if ALL hold: (a) the answer is in the curated answer list for its length; (b) the solver solves it in ≤6 guesses; (c) the answer satisfies the §2 curation rules (checked programmatically on the lists, not per word at runtime); (d) for multi-board variants, all boards' answers are distinct words. Record the rejection tally per gate in tests; it is the tuning signal.
- **Attempts (Nonogram/Mahjong/Klondike pattern):** **100 generation attempts per board, then reject the seed and advance the RNG stream.** Bound attempts; do not add an unbounded generator to the request path. **Exhaustion / daily-seed failure:** the shared daily path already walks seeds — `dailySeedOk(seed, variant)` is an optional contract field (`src/lib/engine/contract.ts:124`, verified at HEAD) and `daily.ts:114-140` walks up to 1,000 seeds and throws `daily_unavailable` (503) if none passes. Word Guess supplies `dailySeedOk` (Klondike pattern, `src/lib/puzzles/klondike-solitaire/logic.ts:1016` + `manifest.ts:153`): it runs the §4 solver gate for the candidate seed's word and returns false on any failure. The variant is fixed ONCE from attempt 0's derivation and never changes across the walk. If no seed passes within 1,000, do NOT serve a raw random word — the daily board for that day is not issued (`daily_unavailable`) and the failure is logged and reported — and that failure must NOT stop `refreshDailyBoards` for other families.
- **Rules encoding (Klondike pattern — specified in this brief, not owner-locked):** `createRound` MUST return exactly these five keys in `rules`, always present, no extra keys, no missing keys — values are safe integers only (the daily response validator accepts only booleans and safe integers, `PlaySession.svelte:390-393`, verified at HEAD `fa6279d`): `length` (4 | 5 | 6 | 7), `boards` (1 | 2 | 4 | 8), `constraint` (0 = normal | 1 = hard-mode | 2 = ultra-hard), `timing` (0 = untimed | 1 = countdown-120 | 2 = per-guess-60), `hint` (0 = none | 1 = budget-3). This encoding uniquely identifies all 11 Batch A variants — classic (5, 1, 0, 0, 0); `hard-mode` (5, 1, 1, 0, 0); `ultra-hard` (5, 1, 2, 0, 0); `length-4` (4, 1, 0, 0, 0); `length-6` (6, 1, 0, 0, 0); `length-7` (7, 1, 0, 0, 0); `boards-2` (5, 2, 0, 0, 0); `boards-4` (5, 4, 0, 0, 0); `boards-8` (5, 8, 0, 0, 0); `countdown-120` (5, 1, 0, 1, 0); `hint-budget-3` (5, 1, 0, 0, 1) — each tuple differs from every other in at least one key. Future batches must preserve uniqueness (a new variant whose rules tuple collides with an existing one is a brief error, not a runtime guess). `encodeRules` lives in `logic.ts` (Klondike pattern, `klondike-solitaire/logic.ts:176`).
- **Move shape:** numeric-only fields only, exact keys fixed in `manifest.parseMove` with exact sorted-key validation like Connect (`'at,cell,pair,stroke'`, verified in `src/lib/puzzles/connect/manifest.ts:108`). Proposed keys, sorted (specified in this brief, not owner-locked): `'action,code'`. Action codes: 0 = guess (`code` = the submitted word encoded base-26, letter A=0 … Z=25, most-significant first; 26^7 fits a safe integer), 1 = hint (`code` 0), 2 = timeout (**RESERVED for per-guess-60, later batches only — Revision 1 fix 4: no Batch A variant uses it; do not build the per-guess clock in v1.4.6**; when built, the 60-second guess clock expiring consumes one guess row with no word — `code` 0). Unused numeric fields must be 0 (never omitted — the sorted-key check requires all keys). Hint is a replayed action (Nonogram action pattern): hint changes only the hint count and score. A guess that is invalid, or that violates the variant's constraint rule, is rejected by the Game layer BEFORE it is logged — it never becomes a move; in replay, such a move throws `RangeError` (tampered log). Replay regenerates the round from the seed, re-applies moves via `applyWordGuessMove` (illegal move throws `RangeError`), and checks the final state: win = every board solved within its budget; loss = budget exhausted with a board unsolved (a loss is a legitimate completed outcome — replay returns score 0, it does NOT throw `incomplete`). **Submission bound (Klondike §8E(a) pattern — verify at HEAD before relying on it):** a Word Guess round can never exceed its guess budget + 3 hints + timeouts (at most ~29 moves), far below the 10,000-move cap, so the completed run is submitted to `/api/progress` under the normal bound. Checkpoints are local (IndexedDB, `src/lib/ui/checkpoint.ts` `saveCheckpoint`) and never cross that request path.

## 5. Scoring, ranked integrity, ending

- **Scoring (`brief-v1`, LOCKED numbers — L4, owner 2026-11-16; admin-changeable any time):** the numbers below are the **default** scoring config; they are **admin-changeable any time** via the admin scoring editor (`brief-v1` immutable versions, §1 item 3) — a later admin change does not need a new brief. Single board: the guess on which the board was solved scores `first/second/third/fourth/fifth/sixth` (100/90/75/60/45/30 for guesses 1–6). **Revision 1 — guesses 7–13 (multi-board only):** a board can be solved on shared guess 7–13 (budgets 7/9/13), which L4's 1–6 scale did not cover — this was Codex's conflict 2. The scale is extended by the formula `max(30 − 5·(n−6), 5)`: seventh 25, eighth 20, ninth 15, tenth 10, eleventh 5, twelfth 5, thirteenth 5. This extension is Revision 1 specified, NOT owner-locked — it is added to the §10 specified-not-locked list (item i) for owner confirmation at re-acceptance. Multi-board: each board scores on the same scale by the guess on which *that* board was solved; total = sum of per-board scores. Hint: −100 per hint used. Countdown-120: +10 per remaining second on a win. Lose (any board unsolved at budget end, X/6–X/13): score 0 — this is the brief's reading of the locked "lose X/6 = 0" for multi-board play, flagged in §10 for owner confirmation at acceptance. Score never goes below zero (hint deduction floored at 0). Per-guess-60: not built in v1.4.6 (Revision 1 fix 4) — no time bonus is specified for it here; its scoring is a later-batch matter. Elapsed seconds for scoring are the active-time seconds `saveRun` computes (`gameplayMs` in `runs.ts`, verified at HEAD `fa6279d`): wall-clock from session creation with the opening time subtracted — the manifest's `rankedTiming.openingMs` (0 ms for Word Guess, §5 timing floor) and `cardOpeningMs`.
