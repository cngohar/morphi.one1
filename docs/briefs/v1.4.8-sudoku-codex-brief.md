## 2. Rules — from global research

Source: global Sudoku research (18 Nov 2026, `workspace/research_notes/sudoku-global-research-20261118/report.md`). The rules below are this brief's own statements; no external file is required to build from them.

- **Core loop:** a 9×9 grid (81 cells) is presented whole on a centered phone board; the player fills the empty cells so every row, every column and every 3×3 box contains digits 1–9 exactly once. The grid has 27 units; each cell belongs to 3 units and has 20 peers. Digits are symbols, not quantities — no arithmetic in classic play.
- **Givens:** pre-filled digits, fixed and unchangeable. Minimum 17 givens for a unique-solution 9×9 puzzle, proven 2012 (McGuire, Tugemann & Civario — no 16-clue unique puzzle exists). Symmetry of givens is presentation only, not a rule and not a variant — the baseline does not require it.
- **Digit replacement (Rev 5, review R22 — normal play, demo behaviour):** placing a digit into a filled player-entered cell REPLACES its digit, under the same rules as placing into an empty cell: a replacement digit that duplicates in its row, column or box (tested with the cell's current digit removed, so the old digit does not conflict with itself) is refused at entry, the old digit stays, and it does NOT count as a mistake; a replacement digit that is conflict-free but wrong against the unique solution is placed, shown in red (never by colour alone), and counts as 1 mistake; a replacement with the correct digit corrects the cell and may complete the board. Replacing a digit is one logged placement (action 0, §4), undoable — Undo restores the previous digit. Re-entering the same digit into a filled cell is a no-op: nothing is logged, no mistake, no message. Givens are never replaceable: a placement into a given cell is refused at entry, never logged, never a mistake. Erase (action 1) clears a player-entered digit; it cannot erase a given. Notes cannot be entered in a filled cell.
- **Uniqueness and logic-only solvability (D7):** a valid puzzle has exactly one solution, and that solution must be reachable by logic alone on the preset's FULL technique ladder (any band — Easy, Medium or Hard techniques for that preset; §4 gate (b)). A unique puzzle that requires guessing or backtracking is rejected at generation and regenerated, at every band. The Easy ladder (naked/hidden singles, locked candidates — SE below ~3) is NOT the generation gate: it is the DAILY eligibility filter only (§4 gate (c)). A board that needs more advanced techniques is a valid unlimited board at its graded band — it is not rejected for failing Easy (Rev 3, review R18).
- **Win:** all 81 cells filled correctly. **Lose:** 3rd solution-error (D4), countdown timeout (§3 #7), or — in no other way. Classic Sudoku has no lose state; the 3-mistake lose is Morphi's rule, not classic play.
- **Conflicts vs solution errors (D4):** a conflict error — entering a digit that duplicates in its row, column or box — is rejected at entry (the cell stays empty, a message is shown, demo-proved) and does NOT count as a mistake. A solution error — a digit that is conflict-free but wrong against the unique solution — is placed, shown in red (never by colour alone: underline/striping or shake as well), and counts as 1 mistake.
- **Undo:** free and unlimited — it steps back placements, note changes and erases, and restores the pre-fill note state after an auto-fill. Undo rolls back the board, not the scoreboard: hints used and mistakes made are NOT refunded.
- **Restart:** a replayed action (§4) that restores the givens-only board, costs −50 (§5), and does NOT reset elapsed ranked time or hints used. Restart does not re-roll the board (same seed → same board). Restart needs confirmation if a game is in progress. Restart restores the givens-only board and NOTHING else — it does NOT reset elapsed ranked time, hints used, the ranked deadline, or the visible countdown clock (Rev 2, review R11 — Mahjong pattern: `mahjongRemainingMs` counts down from the accumulated active time and restart never touches it). The visible clock keeps showing the time remaining against the original deadline, `max(0, 600,000 − gameplayMs)`: a restart at 590 s of play leaves 10 s on the clock and 10 s of play, not a fresh 600 s (§5 deadline rule).
- **Hint (D5):** Show — one solver-backed hint fills the correct digit in a cell. Deterministic: the hint follows the unique solution. Hint budget is unlimited by default (admin-managed cap, §5A).
- **Notes (D11):** pencil-mode toggle on the 1–9 pad; candidates in fixed 3×3 positions inside a cell; auto-cleanup on placement (placing a digit clears that cell's notes and removes that digit from notes in its row, column and box; a digit placed nine times is removed everywhere). Auto-fill-all-candidates is a separate one-tap option, reversible with Undo.
- **Information shown:** row, column and box of the selected cell are highlighted, as are all cells holding the same digit; duplicate/conflicting cells are flagged; the digit pad shows remaining counts and dims a digit placed nine times. Givens are distinguished from player digits by weight/colour, and mistakes are never signalled by colour alone.
- **Controls:** tap-a-cell then tap-a-digit on a 3×3 pad of 1–9 below the board (cell-first is the baseline; digit-first is an input option, not a variant). Keyboard: arrows to move, 1–9 to enter, Backspace/Delete/0 to erase, N to toggle Notes, U or Ctrl+Z to Undo, H for Hint, R for Restart. Digit pad keys are real buttons with spoken labels. Cells are ~40 px at 390 px (D12) with enlarged invisible hit areas; browser pinch-to-zoom stays enabled (D12) — this is the OS/browser gesture, not an in-game control; no in-game board pan/zoom controls are built (§6).
- **Style reference:** Morphi branding is pale `#eef2f7`, navy `#1b2433`, Nunito, orange-red `#e5532d`, blue `#2f6fed`, teal `#009a86`, violet `#7d61ae`, vector only. Demo: soft white board card, pill Time/Mistakes/Hints stats, Undo/Hint flanking a prominent Restart. Do NOT copy any existing game's art, icon or level set. Do NOT add difficulty pills, a tagline, a puzzle-piece logo, side panels, level dots, or a band picker — Morphi has no levels and the band is drawn from the seed (§4).

## 4. Generation, determinism, fairness

- **RNG:** `createRng(seed)` (`src/lib/engine/rng.ts`, SplitMix64 v1 — verified at HEAD `d862095`: `createRng(seed: string)` with `nextU64()`; `shuffle(rng, items)` exported from the same file, used by Connect). All draws from one stream in fixed order. Integer-only. **Banned in logic/manifest:** `Math.random`, `Date.now`, `performance.now`, `new Date`, float math (`Math.sqrt/pow/sin/...`). Enforced by ESLint + `scripts/check-boundaries.ts`; a float solver diverges across the 6 runtimes.
- **Pipeline (generate-then-verify — research §5; fill-then-remove):**
  1. Fill a complete valid 9×9 grid from the seeded PRNG by backtracking (row/column/box masks, 9-bit).
  2. For the preset, place its extra structure from the seed stream against the complete grid: irregular regions first (nine connected 9-cell regions tiling the grid), then cage partition + sums (Killer), thermometer/line shapes, Kropki/XV marks, parity cells — each placed only where the complete grid satisfies the relation. A Thermo line longer than 9 cells is impossible (strictly increasing digits 1–9) — cap line length at 9. A Sandwich clue is placed only where both 1 and 9 exist (later batches).
  3. Remove cells one at a time in seeded order, keeping a removal only if a solution-counting solver still finds exactly one completion (`countSolutions` with limit 2, early exit at the second solution) under the preset's full constraint set, otherwise restore the digit.
  4. Grade the board with the preset's ladder (§4 gates): first the universal gate (logic-only solvable, any band), then — for a daily board only — the Easy eligibility filter. Reject/regenerate with an offset seed on any failure of the gate that applies to that board's destination.
- **Fairness gates (the hard rule — reject and regenerate on any failure):** a board is issued only if ALL hold: (a) exactly one solution under the preset's constraints (countSolutions limit 2 returns 1); (b) **universal gate — logic-only solvable (D7):** the unique solution must be reachable by logic alone on the preset's FULL technique ladder (any band — Easy, Medium or Hard techniques for that preset; Killer: cage-size/sum reasoning; Thermo/Kropki/XV/Diagonal/Irregular/Anti-Knight/Odd-Even: the hardest additional deduction the extra rule forces). A board that requires guessing or backtracking is rejected at every band. This gate is the same for daily and unlimited boards; (c) **daily eligibility filter — Easy only:** a DAILY board must additionally pass its preset's Easy ladder — naked/hidden singles and locked candidates only for classic-rule presets (SE below ~3), plus the preset's own Easy ladder for the extra rule — and be solvable in 5–10 minutes. A board that fails Easy is ineligible for the daily but MAY be issued in unlimited play at its graded band (Medium/Hard) — it is not rejected outright (Rev 2, review R14); (d) **not already solved** — the starting (givens-only) board is not a win; (e) givens ≥ 17 for classic-rule presets (proven minimum, research §1/§4); (f) every placed mark/line/cage is satisfiable by the unique solution and at least one mark is load-bearing where the rule requires it (a Kropki dot whose relation the solution violates is a generator bug, not a variant). Record the rejection tally per gate in tests; it is the tuning signal. **Same-board rule (Rev 2, review R14):** the filter grades the SAME board that issuance, resume and replay derive from the seed — `dailySeedOk` runs this §4 pipeline for the candidate seed, `createRound` derives the board from the seed, and the grade is recomputed from the seed at each step, never stored apart from the board. No separately-stored grade, no second board. **Killer rule for Batch A:** the cage partition is generated from the complete grid and the classic fill-then-remove pass runs WITH the cage constraints in the solver, keeping the remaining givens — this is the demo's behaviour and the Batch A production rule. A givens-free Killer (no givens, opening moves from cage sums only) is a later variant, not this preset.
- **Uniqueness is REQUIRED — verification must accept the one solution:** exactly one legal completion exists; the win is "all 81 cells filled correctly against the unique solution", not "find the one canonical trail". Win is judged from player state against the stored solution, never by matching a witness. The solution is stored for tests and for the hint definition only.

- **Move shape:** numeric-only fields only, exact keys fixed in `manifest.parseMove` with exact sorted-key validation like Connect (`'at,cell,pair,stroke'`, verified in `src/lib/puzzles/connect/manifest.ts:107`). Proposed keys, sorted (specified in this brief, not owner-locked): `'action,cell,value'`. Action codes: 0 = place (`cell` 0–80, `value` 1–9 — a placement into an empty cell fills it; a placement into a filled player-entered cell REPLACES its digit (Rev 5, review R22 — normal play, §2), tested with the cell's current digit removed so it does not conflict with itself; givens are never replaceable — a placement into a given cell is refused at entry, never logged, never a mistake; re-entering the same digit is a no-op, never logged; a conflict placement is rejected by the Game layer BEFORE it is logged and never becomes a move; a solution-error placement IS logged and counts as 1 mistake), 1 = erase (`value` 0), 2 = note toggle (`value` 1–9 — adds/removes one candidate in the cell), 3 = undo (pops the last committed move; `cell`/`value` 0; undo never crosses a restart — if the last logged action is a restart, undo does nothing; undo reverses board state only, never score deductions), 4 = hint (`cell`/`value` 0 — fills the correct digit in the solver-chosen cell), 5 = timeout (countdown only, `cell`/`value` 0; replay returns `won: false`, score 0 — ONLY if the timeout is legitimate, i.e. `600 <= seconds <= 602` on the measured call; see §5 deadline rule. A timeout with `seconds = 0` always throws `RangeError('timeout')` as premature, and a timeout with `0 < seconds < 600` throws `RangeError('timeout')`. Rev 0 allowed `seconds = 0` "on the saver's first call" — removed in Rev 1 (review R3): replay receives only `seconds` and cannot tell a preflight call from a measured call, so a seconds-0 allowance cannot be scoped to the first call; and there IS no first call for timeout logs, because the saver skips the preflight replay for them (§8)), 6 = restart (`cell`/`value` 0; restores the givens-only board, score −50, elapsed time and hints used do NOT reset, ranked deadline does NOT move, visible countdown clock does NOT rewind — §2/§5, Rev 2 review R11), 7 = autofill-notes (`cell`/`value` 0 — fills all candidates in every empty cell; reversible with Undo, which restores the pre-fill note state). Hint/undo/restart/timeout/autofill are replayed actions (Nonogram action pattern). Replay regenerates the round from the seed, re-applies moves via `applySudokuMove` (illegal move throws `RangeError`), and checks the final state: win = all 81 cells filled correctly; loss = 3rd solution-error reached, countdown timeout, or — no other loss. A loss is a legitimate completed outcome — replay returns `won: false` and score 0, it does NOT throw `incomplete`. Replay returns `won` (contract field already landed, `contract.ts:22`), `hintUsed` from the session-wide sticky flag when a hint move is in the log (Klondike pattern), and `mistakes` = solution-errors on the final state (specified in this brief, not owner-locked). **Move cap (Rev 2, review R13 — `runs.ts:89-94` throws `RunError('moves')` when the log exceeds `maxMoves`):** a Sudoku round's placements are bounded (~81), but repeatable note toggles, undos and restarts are not — the log CAN reach 10,000. **The final slot is reserved for a TERMINAL action only:** move 10,000 may only be a placement that completes the board (win), a placement that is the 3rd solution-error (loss), or a timeout (countdown only) — NEVER a restart or a non-terminal placement. The Game therefore disables ALL non-terminal input once the log reaches 9,999 moves — note toggles, undo, autofill-notes, restart, erase, and any placement/hint that would not end the round; only a terminal action may be logged as move 10,000, and any other input at 9,999 is refused with a message and logged as nothing. A log over 10,000 is never built by the Game. **Cap outcome — no terminal action available (Rev 3, review R16; check corrected Rev 4, review R19; scope corrected Rev 5, review R22):** the terminal-only final slot still strands one state: an UNTIMED round with `mistakeLimit` 0 (unlimited, §5A) and more than one cell still incorrect (empty, or holding a wrong digit) has NO permitted move that can end the round — a correct placement or a hint fills or corrects one cell only, and a wrong placement costs points but never ends the run. (The countdown case does not strand: a timeout is always available as the terminal action, so this state is untimed-only. With a finite `mistakeLimit`, a wrong placement that reaches the limit IS a terminal action — a loss — but ONLY if that wrong placement is loggable; see the check below.) When the log reaches 9,999 moves, the Game must test ACTUAL endability — it must NOT assume a wrong entry is loggable, and it must NOT scan empty cells only. The cap check evaluates the same editable cells and legal actions as normal play: ALL editable cells (empty cells AND player-filled cells — givens are never editable), each candidate placement evaluated exactly as a normal placement would be (for a player-filled cell, the cell's current digit is removed before the conflict test, so it does not conflict with itself; §2 digit replacement). A terminal action exists ONLY if (a) a placement completes the board (win — exactly one empty cell remains and its correct digit is placeable, OR exactly one cell holds a wrong digit and replacing it with the correct digit completes the board), or (b) there EXISTS a legal loggable placement that is a solution-error AND reaches the mistake limit — i.e. an editable cell + digit (empty, or player-filled with its current digit removed for the test) that is conflict-free under EVERY entry-refusal rule (unit conflicts, and the preset's own refusal rules: region/diagonal/knight/parity/cage as applicable), wrong against the unique solution, AND mistakes are at limit−1 — or (c) a countdown timeout is available. "Mistakes at limit−1" alone is NOT enough (Rev 3's check was wrong): if two cells remain and each accepts only its correct digit, every wrong entry is refused as a conflict (§4 action 0 / D4 — conflicts are refused at entry, never logged, never a mistake), so no loggable wrong placement exists and neither correct entry wins. The Game knows the solution, the board, the refusal rules and the mistake count, so it can test (a) and (b) directly by scanning all editable cells × digits 1–9, evaluating each candidate exactly as normal play would. If none of (a)–(c) holds at 9,999 logged moves, the Game offers the existing "Try another puzzle" action (`v1.nav.another`, the `ontryanother` prop the shared stage already passes to every Game; Klondike `Game.svelte:983`, Light Bend `Game.svelte:778`, Mahjong `Game.svelte:887` render the same button when blocked). For a ranked daily the attempt IS spent: a new ranked start for a session whose `abandoned_at` is set throws `daily_spent` (409) (`ranked-session.ts:345`), so the redeal is the next puzzle in the normal flow, not a replacement daily. ## 5. Scoring, ranked integrity, ending

- **Scoring (`brief-v1`, DEFAULTS — D6, owner 2026-11-19; admin-changeable any time):** the numbers below are the **default** scoring config; they are **admin-changeable any time** via the admin scoring editor (`brief-v1` immutable versions, §1 item 3, §5A) — a later admin change does not need a new brief. Win: `score = max(floor, completion − second × seconds) − hint × hintsUsed − mistake × mistakes − restart × restarts`, floored at 0. A 5-minute Easy solve scores 700 before deductions; a 10-minute solve scores 400; beyond ~13 minutes the time component floors at 200. The −1/sec rate is deliberate: Light Bend's −4/sec rate would collapse every 5–10 minute Easy solve to the floor (300 s × 4 = 1,200 > 1,000) and destroy time discrimination. Do NOT copy the −4/sec rate. Countdown-600: `score = completion + countdown × remainingSeconds − hint × hintsUsed − mistake × mistakes − restart × restarts`, floored at 0; no −1/sec deduction (to avoid double-counting time). A 600-second countdown solved in 300 s scores 1,300 before deductions; solved in 590 s scores 1,010. Timeout = run over — a completed loss, score 0, no board row (D8; see Loss handling). Undo is free (it reverses the move taken back, never its score deduction). No partial credit: an unfinished or lost board scores 0 and (for a loss) saves no row. Per-cell scoring is NOT used — a Sudoku win is completing the grid, not filling cells one by one. Elapsed seconds for scoring are the active-time seconds `saveRun` computes (`gameplayMs` in `runs.ts`, verified at HEAD `d862095`): wall-clock from session creation with the opening time subtracted — the manifest's `rankedTiming.openingMs` (6,000 ms for Sudoku, §5 timing floor) and `cardOpeningMs` (1,200 ms, §5 timing floor). The untimed path additionally subtracts the shared 1,000 ms wall allowance (`rankedWallMs` in `runs.ts:346`); the timed path subtracts `openingMs` and `cardOpeningMs` from `rankedElapsedMs` directly (`runs.ts:371`). The shared 1,000 ms wall allowance is RETAINED: changing it would shift every family's ranked timing and scoring and is out of scope — do not change it here.
