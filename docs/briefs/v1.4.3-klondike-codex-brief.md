## 2. Rules — from global research

Source: global Klondike Solitaire research (9 Nov 2026, `workspace/research_notes/klondike-solitaire-global-research-20261109-1205/report.md`). The rules below are this brief's own statements; no external file is required to build from them.

- **Core loop:** move cards between tableau, waste and foundations to expose face-down tableau cards and free stock cards, until every card is on its foundation. Solo card puzzle.
- **The deal:** 7 tableau columns, dealt 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 cards = 28 cards. Only the top card of each column is face up; the other 21 are face down. The remaining 24 cards form the face-down stock, with a waste pile beside it and four foundations.
- **Tableau building:** down by one rank in alternating colours — e.g. a black 5 on a red 6. A properly built run of face-up cards can be moved together as a unit, and part of a run can also be moved.
- **Foundations:** four foundations, one per suit, each starting with an Ace and built up in suit order Ace to King (13 cards each).
- **Exposing cards:** when the last face-up card leaves a tableau column, the face-down card beneath it is turned face up and becomes available. The flip is automatic — it is not a move and it is not replayed as one.
- **Empty columns:** only a King, or a run starting with a King, may fill an empty tableau column (baseline; `any-card` changes this, §3).
- **Stock / waste:** only the top card of the waste pile is playable, to tableau or foundation. Draw 1 turns one stock card at a time; Draw 3 turns three at a time and only the top one is playable. When the stock is empty, the whole waste pile is flipped over without shuffling to form a new stock, subject to the pass limit (§3, Axis B).
- **Redeal limits vary by version — there is no single canonical limit:** unlimited passes are the computer-era default; other versions allow 1 redeal, 3 redeals, no redeals, or 1 pass in Draw 1 / 3 passes in Draw 3 under Vegas scoring. The baseline is unlimited passes (matrix §0).
- **Win:** all 52 cards moved to the four foundations. **Lose (run over):** no legal moves remain in tableau or waste and the stock cannot be reset or its redeals are exhausted, even if cards remain. Classic paper Klondike has no time limit and no forced moves; a card does not have to be played to a foundation as soon as it can be.
- **Foundation move-back:** allowed in the baseline (matrix §0 locks the computer-version rule; D7 LOCKED by the owner, 2026-11-14). A card taken back off a foundation loses the foundation points it earned (§5).
- **Cards:** one standard 52-card deck, no jokers. Card identity is an integer 0–51: suit = `floor(id / 13)`, rank = `id % 13 + 1` (suit order fixed in `logic.ts`: 0 = spades, 1 = hearts, 2 = diamonds, 3 = clubs). Integer-only, everywhere.
- **Controls (demo-proved — see header):** tap a card to send it to its best legal spot; drag-and-drop (real pointer drag CONFIRMED); double-tap a card to send it home (NOT VERIFIED live — §9 item); tap-to-select then tap-destination as the fallback on phones; keyboard Tab enters the board, arrows move focus, Enter/Space selects or plays, Esc clears selection, H = Hint, U = Undo, R = Restart (same seed, same deal); Hint, Undo, Auto-finish, Restart, New Deal buttons. Auto-finish is enabled exactly when the deterministic greedy sweep succeeds (§8C(g), locked): repeatedly move any exposed card (top tableau card or top waste card) that is the next rank of its foundation, in any order, until no such move exists; if the sweep empties tableau + waste (all 52 home), Auto-finish is enabled and performs that sweep — all cards face up is necessary but NOT sufficient, and the sweep must not use foundation move-back. If the sweep stalls with cards remaining, Auto-finish is disabled and refuses. Restart replays the same seed and the same deal — it must never re-roll; Restart is a replayed action (code 6, §4, §8C(c)): it resets the board to the initial deal, score to 0 and hints used to 0, clears the undo stack, and does NOT reset elapsed ranked time or consume a new attempt. Restart is available during play INCLUDING in `no-undo` (restart is not undo). The demo's "Redeal" wording is banned here: recycling the exhausted stock and dealing a completely new game are different actions and must be named differently (research §5).
- **Three distinct actions — do not conflate them (Amendment 1, 2026-11-14; the pre-amendment brief wrongly equated the R key with New Deal while describing Restart's same-seed behaviour): Restart** = same seed, same deal, never re-rolls (keyboard R). **New Deal** = abandon the current round and start a fresh deal in the same family and the same variant, with a new seed (see §8B(a) — this needs a new `onnewdeal` prop; the existing `ontryanother` cannot do it). If the abandoned round was the daily ranked attempt, New Deal consumes the first attempt per D2 and saves no `daily_best` row. **Try Another (run-over)** = abandon the failed round and start another puzzle via the selector, unchanged (`ontryanother`, `PlaySession.tryAnother()`).
- **Demo to copy:** artifact `klondike-solitaire-demo` (daily Draw 1, Draw 3 switch, Quick-finish test deal — 48 home, 4 Kings left — reachable only from the Restart menu under a labelled banner). **Use the demo as the visual and behaviour reference only — do not copy its code as production logic (demo RNG is not SplitMix64; demo deals are seeded random shuffles, NOT solver-checked; demo has no ranked timing, server replay or daily board).** **DO NOT COPY the demo's bottom Draw 1 / Draw 3 segmented control into the app — this is the Tangram Square/Row lesson: Codex copied the demo's Square/Row tray literally into Tangram and Row then had to be removed (Amendment 5). Draw 1 vs Draw 3 is a rule variant selected by the variant system / daily board, never a player-facing toggle; the demo's bottom bar is a demo picker only. In production there is NO Draw 1/3 toggle on screen; the round-rule line (§5) states the draw mode for that round only. The demo's Restart-menu Quick-finish test deal is likewise demo-only and must not ship as a player option.** If the demo cannot be opened, STOP and report.

## 4. Generation, determinism, fairness

- **RNG:** `createRng(seed)` (`src/lib/engine/rng.ts`, SplitMix64 v1 — verified at HEAD `80c270e`: `createRng(seed: string)` with `nextU64()`; `shuffle(rng, items)` exported from the same file, used by Connect). All draws from one stream in fixed order. Integer-only. **Banned in logic/manifest:** `Math.random`, `Date.now`, `performance.now`, `new Date`, float math (`Math.sqrt/pow/sin/...`). Enforced by ESLint + `scripts/check-boundaries.ts`; a float solver diverges across the 6 runtimes.
- **Deal:** seeded shuffle of cards 0–51 via `createRng` + `shuffle` → deal 1–7 cards to tableau columns 1–7 (28 total, top card of each column face up), remaining 24 to the stock. The deal order is fixed: row by row, left to right (column 1 gets 1 card, column 2 gets 2, …) — the same order the demo's deal-in animation follows.
- **Pipeline (generate-and-filter — the standard guarantee, research §4):** shuffle a deal, run the solver, serve it only if the solver finds a winning line under that variant's own rules; otherwise discard and re-deal from the advanced RNG stream. There is **no verified Klondike reverse-dealing construction** (research §4) — do not claim Mahjong's reverse-deal method as Klondike's. A variant solver check must be run under that variant's rules (a classic winnable deal is NOT certified for `pass-1`, `same-colour` or `different-suit`).
- **Solver (NEW — no reuse point):** no Klondike solver exists in the repo (verified by search at HEAD `80c270e`; the only solver in the repo is Nonogram's line-solver in `src/lib/puzzles/nonogram/logic.ts`, which is the structural pattern only — enumerate/check/reject — not the algorithm). Codex writes a deterministic, integer-only Klondike solver in `logic.ts`. It must handle the full move set (tableau runs, waste, foundations, foundation move-back, recycles within the variant's pass limit) and return one of three honest outcomes: **won** (a winning line found), **proved dead** (no winning line exists), or **undecided within budget**. Only **won** issues a deal; proved-dead and undecided both reject and regenerate. Record the solver budget and the undecided share in tests; never present a solver floor as the true winnability rate. The solver runs inside `createRound` (generation path) and in server replay (which regenerates the round from the seed), so it must be bounded and fast enough to keep round creation interactive — the budget is D5 LOCKED (100 attempts, §4 below) **plus a deterministic finite per-deal solver budget of 400,000 nodes per deal (LOCKED — Amendment 2, §8C(f); calibration, research §4: 60.8% Draw 1 / 51.0% Draw 3 proved at 8 s / 400,000 nodes — nodes, not wall time, because the budget must be deterministic across runtimes)**. A deal whose solver exceeds 400,000 nodes without a winning line is **undecided** (not proved dead). 100 attempts × 400,000 nodes is the maximum work per deal generation. The solver must report nodes used per deal in tests; the budget is nodes, never wall time. Do NOT put an unbounded solver in the request path. **For v1.4.3 a daily deal must be solver-winnable only — NO additional "winnable within a casual move/time budget" requirement (D5 LOCKED; no casual-budget definition exists yet; revisit after the solver exists).**
- **Fairness gate (the hard rule):** a solver-proven-winnable deal only — never a raw random deal. Roughly 18% of random Klondike deals are unwinnable (research §4/§8); the thoughtful ceiling is ~90.5% Draw 1 / ~81.9% Draw 3 with perfect play (Blake & Gent), while human win rates are far lower (~35% Draw 1 / ~15% Draw 3 in one large player sample). A budget-limited filter skews toward easier deals, because hard winnable deals are more likely to time out undecided — record the skew, do not hide it.
- **Attempts (Nonogram/Mahjong pattern):** **100 solver attempts per deal, then reject the seed and advance the RNG stream** (D5 LOCKED, owner 2026-11-14). Require ≥1 legal move at deal time (no dead opening — unplayable deals are ~0.25% of shuffles, research §4). Bound solver attempts; do not add an exact solver to the request path beyond `createRound`/replay. **Exhaustion / daily-seed failure (LOCKED — Amendment 2, §8C(f)):** if all 100 attempts on a seed are proved-dead or undecided, reject the seed and advance the RNG stream. Daily pre-generation at 00:00 UTC walks seeds deterministically from the date seed until a solver-won deal is found, bounded to **1,000 seeds**; the variant is fixed ONCE from attempt 0's derivation and never changes across the walk (§8E(d)); if no solver-won deal is found within 1,000 seeds, do NOT serve a raw random deal — the daily board for that day is not issued (`daily_unavailable`) and the failure is logged and reported — and that failure must NOT stop `refreshDailyBoards` for other families (§8E(c)). (Implemented in `dailyPuzzle`, `src/lib/server/daily.ts`, per §8D(a) — the walk was not in the pre-amendment code; see §8D(a) for the derivation scheme and the `dailySeedOk` hook.)
- **Rules encoding (LOCKED — Amendment 2, 2026-11-14; required for New Deal variant recovery, §8C(b)):** `createRound` MUST return exactly these seven keys in `rules`, always present, no extra keys, no missing keys — values are booleans or safe integers only (the daily response validator accepts only those types, `PlaySession.svelte:370-400`): `draw` (1 | 3), `passes` (0 = unlimited | 1 | 3), `anyCard` (boolean), `thoughtful` (boolean), `build` (0 = alternating-colour | 1 = same-colour | 2 = different-suit), `undo` (boolean), `hintBudget` (0 = on-demand | 3). This encoding uniquely identifies all 11 Batch A variants — classic (1, 0, false, false, 0, true, 0); `draw-3` (3, 0, false, false, 0, true, 0); `pass-3` (1, 3, false, false, 0, true, 0); `pass-1` (1, 1, false, false, 0, true, 0); `any-card` (1, 0, true, false, 0, true, 0); `thoughtful` (1, 0, false, true, 0, true, 0); `same-colour` (1, 0, false, false, 1, true, 0); `different-suit` (1, 0, false, false, 2, true, 0); `no-undo` (1, 0, false, false, 0, false, 0); `hint-budget-3` (1, 0, false, false, 0, true, 3); `draw-3-pass-3` (3, 3, false, false, 0, true, 0) — each tuple differs from every other in at least one key. Future batches must preserve uniqueness (a new variant whose rules tuple collides with an existing one is a brief error, not a runtime guess).
- **Move shape:** numeric-only fields only, exact keys fixed in `manifest.parseMove` with exact sorted-key validation like Connect (`'at,cell,pair,stroke'`, verified in `src/lib/puzzles/connect/manifest.ts:108`). Proposed keys, sorted (specified in this brief, not owner-locked): `'action,at,card,count,dst,src'`. Pile codes (fixed in `logic.ts`): stock 0, waste 1, foundations 2–5 (spades, hearts, diamonds, clubs — same suit order as card ids), tableau columns 6–12. Action codes: 0 = draw, 1 = move, 2 = recycle, 3 = hint, 4 = undo, 5 = auto-finish, 6 = restart (Amendment 2, §8C(c)). For a move, `card` is the lowest card of the moved run and `count` is the run length; for a draw, `card` is the drawn card id and `count` is the draw count (1 or 3); unused numeric fields must be 0 (never omitted — the sorted-key check requires all six keys). **Partial Draw 3 (LOCKED — Amendment 2):** when the stock holds fewer cards than the draw count, the draw moves ALL remaining stock cards to waste. Recorded fields: `count` = the actual number drawn (1 or 2 in Draw 3; Draw 1 is always 1 while the stock is non-empty), `card` = the top waste card after the draw (the last card drawn, the playable one); `at`/`dst`/`src` are 0 for a draw. The waste shows up to 3 cards, only the top one is playable. If the stock is empty there is no draw action at all — it is a recycle (action 2) if passes remain, else no action (a draw on an empty stock throws `RangeError`). Hint/undo/auto-finish are replayed action codes (Nonogram `NONOGRAM_ACTION` pattern): hint changes only the hint count and score; undo pops the last committed move and reverses its score delta; auto-finish re-derives the home sweep deterministically from the revealed state. **Restart (action 6, §8C(c)):** for a restart all other numeric fields are 0. On a restart action, replay resets the board to the initial deal (same seed), score to 0, `hintsUsed` to 0 (the per-attempt counter — a fresh attempt at the same deal; pre-restart hint penalties are discarded with the score; the session-wide sticky flag `hintUsedSession` is NEVER reset by Restart, and replay's `hintUsed` result flag scans the FULL move log, §8D(b); this deliberately differs from Nonogram's restart, `NONOGRAM_ACTION.restart` = 11, which preserves spent penalties), and clears the undo stack (undo cannot cross a restart boundary — an undo action after a restart with an empty history throws `RangeError`). Elapsed ranked time does NOT reset (server wall-clock runs from session creation, `runs.ts`). `maxMoves` counts ALL moves appended in the session log cumulatively, including restart actions themselves (each restart action counts as 1 move toward the 10,000 cap). Restart is available during play INCLUDING in `no-undo` (restart is not undo — action 4 is refused there, action 6 is allowed). Restart on a ranked daily does NOT consume a new attempt (same deal, same attempt — it neither consumes nor renews it). The Game MUST append the restart action via `onmove`/`checkpointMove` like every other action, so server replay sees it — a board-only reset that appends nothing would leave replay out of sync and is forbidden. The expose flip is automatic and is never a move. Replay regenerates the round from the seed, re-applies moves via `applyKlondikeMove` (illegal move throws `RangeError`), and checks the final state is all 52 cards on the foundations. The solver's winning line is stored for tests only — never used to judge a player's solution (Connect comment pattern). Win is judged from player state. **Submission bound (§8E(a)):** a full 10,000-move log serialises to ~560 KB (one 6-field move is 55 bytes JSON), so the completed run is submitted to `/api/progress` under a 1,048,576-byte (1 MiB) bound — the 65,536-byte play-request cap cannot carry it (§8E(a)). Checkpoints are local (IndexedDB, `src/lib/ui/checkpoint.ts` `saveCheckpoint`) and never cross that request path, so checkpoint save is unaffected by the cap. Daily resume DOES cross a request path — it sends the full move log to the leaderboard daily action — and is covered by the §8F(a) 1 MiB bound on both layers (`actionRequest` + `readPlayBody`); §8E(a)'s claim that daily resume "does not hit the cap" was wrong and is corrected by §8F(a).

## 5. Scoring, ranked integrity, ending

- **Scoring (`brief-v1`, LOCKED default — D3, owner 2026-11-14; admin-changeable any time):** Standard Microsoft scoring from the research and the demo — NOT a Morphi invention. The numbers below are the **default** scoring config; they are **admin-changeable any time** via the admin scoring editor (`brief-v1` immutable versions, §1 item 3) — a later admin change does not need a new brief. `score = 5 × (waste → tableau) + 10 × (any card → foundation) + 5 × (tableau card flipped face up) − 15 × (foundation → tableau) − recyclePenalty + timeBonus − 20 × hintsUsed (hint-budget-3 only)`, floored at 0. Recycle penalty (clarified, Amendment 1, 2026-11-14 — numbers unchanged): −100 per recycle in Draw 1; −20 per recycle in Draw 3, but only from the 3rd recycle onward, i.e. from the start of the 4th pass — the first three passes are penalty-free, so recycles 1 and 2 (which start passes 2 and 3) cost nothing. In a `pass-3` variant (3 passes total, §3) no 4th pass exists, so no Draw 3 recycle penalty ever applies there; in unlimited variants the penalty applies per recycle as stated. (research §2; demo uses this.) The timed −2 per 10 seconds is EXCLUDED (D3(c)) — do not add it. No Vegas money presentation (D3(d)) — Vegas counts only through its pass limit. Routing a card via the tableau first scores +5 then +10 (+15 total) vs +10 direct to foundation — that is the wild rule, keep it, and the brief states it. Moving between tableau columns scores nothing by itself. Score never goes below zero. Maximum Standard score without time bonus: 745. Draw 1 tends to produce higher winning scores than Draw 3 because more cards are accessible — that is fine, because the daily board is per (family, date) with one variant per day, so scores never mix across variants.
- **Time bonus (re-expressed to fit the scoring engine — read this):** research gives the bonus as **700,000 ÷ seconds to finish**, only if the game takes at least 30 seconds; under 30 seconds no bonus. `src/lib/engine/scoring.ts` (verified at HEAD) requires every `ScoreDefinition` field value to be a safe integer in 0..10000 (`SCORE_MAX = 10000`), so the literal 700,000 cannot be a field. ### §8C(d) — Partial Draw 3 (LOCKED)

**Why:** the §4 move shape permitted draw counts of only 1 or 3. ### §8C(f) — Solver node budget: 400,000 nodes per deal (LOCKED)

**Why:** the D5 100-attempt limit bounds shuffled deals but sets no per-deal solver node budget — without one, a single deal could run unbounded inside `createRound`. **Locked:** a deterministic finite per-deal solver budget of **400,000 nodes per deal** (calibration, research §4: 60.8% Draw 1 / 51.0% Draw 3 proved at 8 s / 400,000 nodes — nodes, not wall time, because the budget must be deterministic across runtimes). ### §8C(g) — Auto-finish eligibility: greedy sweep, not just face-up (LOCKED)

**Why:** §2 said Auto-finish sweeps home "once all cards are face up" — but all cards face up does not necessarily mean a direct legal sweep to foundations is available (a face-up card may be blocked behind a foundation order its suit cannot yet take, or the waste top may not be homeable while a tableau card is). **Locked rule (§2):** Auto-finish is enabled IFF the deterministic greedy sweep succeeds — repeatedly move any exposed card (top tableau card or top waste card) that is the next rank of its foundation, in any order, until no such move exists; if the sweep empties tableau + waste (all 52 home), Auto-finish is enabled and performs that sweep; if the sweep stalls with cards remaining, Auto-finish is disabled and refuses. ### §8C(h) — Thoughtful entrance: face-up flight, no flip (LOCKED)

**Why:** the `thoughtful` variant reveals every tableau card, while the §7 entrance specifies flips for the top card of each column — a face-down flight followed by a flip would briefly hide information the variant promises to reveal. **Locked rule (§7 rule 4a):** in the `thoughtful` variant all 28 cards fly in FACE-UP (rank and suit visible throughout the flight) with NO flip step — the 180 ms in-place flip is skipped for Thoughtful.
