The baseline

Mahjong Solitaire, in four moves

This research concerns the solo stacked-tile game, also called Shanghai. Four-player hand-building Mahjong belongs to a different game family.

01 · DEAL

Tiles form a layered layout. Covered faces may be partly or fully hidden.

02 · FIND FREE TILES

Nothing may cover a tile, and at least one long side must be open.

03 · MATCH A PAIR

Remove matching free tiles. Standard Flowers and Seasons match within their own groups.

04 · OPEN THE BOARD

Clear every tile. Without recovery aids, a board with tiles left and no legal pair is blocked.

Baseline: Goodsol's tile-matching rules. Implementations differ; this is the comparison baseline, not a universal software specification.

The counting rule

Count each kind of variety on its own terms

The earlier variant reports emphasise rule changes. This report keeps the same structure and adds a broader taxonomy suited to Mahjong. Each catalogue entry has one primary dimension and an explicit evidence type; entries can overlap and must not be added together as unique games.

PLAY VARIETY More than rules alone

  • Rules & mechanicsWhat may match, which edges open a tile, what a move changes and what ends a round.
  • Structural variantsTile count, depth, overlap, row lengths, disconnected stacks and the graph of blockers. These can change strategy under identical removal rules.
  • Mode & policy variantsCountdowns, target objectives, score systems, aids, fair-deal guarantees, daily attempts and competitive formats.

KEEP SEPARATE Avoid inflated totals

  • Cosmetic themesArtwork, sound and backgrounds affect readability or feel. Under Morphi's contract, they never change legality, difficulty or score.
  • New seedsNew deals are replay content within a variant, not automatically new variants.
  • Duplicate names & productsTwo publishers may use the same layout name for different geometry, or different names for equivalent geometry. Counts here are not a deduplicated world registry.

The quality gate

Five checks before calling a combination a useful variant

Does the decision change?A new layout should change meaningful dependencies, not only move an unchanged board around.

Is the challenge understandable?Show why a tile is blocked and explain any new win condition without a long tutorial.

Can it be solved fairly?Validate the layout and generated deal. A solvable starting position does not make every legal player choice safe.

Can it be played on a phone?144 tiny targets can fail even when a screenshot fits. Geometry, hit regions, layers and zoom need direct review.

Can scores be compared?Different assistance, timers, objectives or difficulty distributions require a deliberate ranking policy.

Data versus new mechanics

Same core can support variety — each axis still has a cost

These are Morphi planning judgments, not measured effort estimates or a code audit. Each item still needs generation, replay, accessibility and responsive evidence.

CORE REUSE Data and configuration

  • Layout records — explicit positions, layers, covers and side blockers. More data still means more solvability and sizing work.
  • Face composition — match-group ids, vocabulary and copy counts, with parity and solution validation.
  • Clock / score / objective — reusable policies only after the family has authoritative implementations and separate ranking scopes.

NEW BEHAVIOR State and interaction

  • Wind direction, shuffle tiles, boosters — new transitions, move records, explanations and replay cases.
  • Daily attempts and campaigns — scheduling, persistence and eligibility work beyond a new board file.
  • Competitive or 3D play — coordination or rendering/input architecture; not a small theme option.
For v1.3.4: this research supports layout-driven growth and a wider future palette of mechanics. It does not promote the whole catalogue into the build. The current proposal, its acceptance gates and owner-directed verification schedule remain separate.

Family boundaries

Related Mahjong-branded games deserve their own labels

A broad discovery catalogue can include these branches. A strict count of stacked pair-removal variants should show them separately. These classifications are editorial judgments; publishers often market all of them as “Mahjong”.

What remains uncertain

The limits are part of the research

  • No exhaustive world registry. The 28 entries are a curated research set, not a census or a mathematical maximum.
  • Layout names are not specifications. Turtle and Fortress need exact coordinates, face distribution and freedom tests before implementation. The local visual draft is not such a specification.
  • Counts overlap. One released game may combine countdown, combos, assists and several layouts. The catalogue groups by main factor rather than pretending entries are disjoint.
  • Version differences matter. Microsoft modes are publisher-confirmed; detailed Lightning/Match/Score rules are community walkthrough evidence, not a tested current binary.
  • Historical multiplayer is historical evidence. Shanghai manuals prove published formats, not that their online services work today.
  • Generation study is limited. Meow-Jong's experiment uses its own exact-face distribution, finite samples and an explicit search budget. It is not a universal solvability percentage for every Mahjong product.
  • No popularity ranking. No reliable per-variant replay or retention comparison was established. Suggested Morphi fit is design judgment.
  • No silent plan changes. Neither source data, research examples nor proposed combinations accept the v1.3.4 game or authorize implementation/deployment.
