## Retention and deletion facts

See the concise inventory in `PRIVACY_RETENTION.md` for each table/category, controlling settings, deletion behavior and provider limits. Account data and all saved rounds persist while the account exists; “30 recent rounds” is a display limit. Sessions expire after seven days with renewal, but expiry is not a physical-erasure timer. Separate OAuth state, rate-limit entries, moderation audits, request correspondence, local browser data and provider copies can survive account deletion.

Local maintenance prunes expired sessions/state, auth limiter entries idle over a configured 24-hour buffer, and safely disposable expired ranked starts. It clears ranked IP hashes outside the one-hour cap window. Claimed-but-uncommitted starts remain recoverable. No cleanup scheduler, audit TTL, inactivity deletion or provider backup-expiry setting is invented. Production operations must decide how maintenance is invoked/monitored before public accounts.

Better Auth's Google authorization URL is tested for exactly `openid`, `email`, `profile` and `access_type=online`; no password registration or Gmail/Drive/Calendar permissions. Its pinned implementation encrypts access/refresh tokens, while the stored ID token is a separate field; the policy does not claim all stored credentials have application-level encryption. Google basic identity claims do not establish player age.

## General audience and reports about children

The owner clarified that child visitors being able to use guest play is not an intention to design/market for children. The actual games are neutral Memory and Block puzzles; there is no child-specific educational content, chat, user uploads, ads or GA4 in this build. This review does not infer that visual simplicity/colors alone determine a legal audience classification. No guest age investigation or separate kids product is introduced.

Guest play still processes connection/provider information. `PRIVACY_REQUESTS.md` specifies how the owner investigates credible reports, establishes reasonable authority without excessive evidence collection, applies the appropriate deletion/restriction/legal response and explains residual provider/device data. The local privacy-delete action supports verified cases; production operator access still needs separate preparation. Do not silently turn a ranked ban into a claim that login or all data collection is blocked.

The public page truthfully states there is no child-account or parental-consent registration service and no independent guest-age verification. It makes no claim that Google filters children, that an age gate exists, or that all reports result in automatic deletion. Public account eligibility and jurisdiction-specific legal questions remain explicit v1 decisions; wording approval is not a legal classification/certification.
